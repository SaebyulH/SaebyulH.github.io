Linguistics / Languages
In 12th grade, I figured that since I had been studying Latin since 9th grade and found it so fun, I could also try taking the Spanish AP test to see if I could pass. I studied historical Latin to spanish linguistic shifts (rather than do a normal Spanish course) and after a few months somehow passed with a 3. Since then, my Spanish has become way better than my Latin since there are much better resources for Spanish and it turns out it's pretty hard to practice speaking Latin with people. As a Korean heritage speaker, I am now doing this for Japanese, since it is very similar structurally to Korean.
