Hello. I am Saebyul Hong. I make 3D models, small games, short films, and long detours through language. This page is an honest inventory of all four, plus one thing I cannot do at all.

I.  Things I can do

BLENDER

  

I have used it since eighth grade—long enough that I know my way around it. Film VFX, game assets, 3D printing, graphic design, video editing: I have done all of them in Blender, largely because I would rather not use Adobe products.

GODOT

  

I am making games and small experiments in Godot. There is more to show as it becomes worth showing.

LINGUISTICS
LANGUAGES

  

I came to this sideways. I had been studying Latin since ninth grade, and in twelfth I decided that instead of taking a normal Spanish course, I would study Spanish–Latin linguistic shifts. A few months later I passed the AP Spanish exam with a 3. Since then my Spanish has overtaken my Latin: there are better resources for Spanish, and it is much easier to find someone to speak it with. I am a Korean heritage speaker, and I am now doing the same thing with Japanese, which is structurally close to Korean.

VIDEO EDITING

  

I have made a handful of short films, enough that I can do the work. I do less of it now.

FLUTTER / DART

  

I made a few apps in Flutter in high school and put them on Google Play. Google later deleted my developer account for inactivity, which was annoying. I will probably return to it after I learn Japanese.

II.  Things I cannot do
